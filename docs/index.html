Why Chronote?

🔄

Automatic Tracking

Runs silently in the background, recording every app you use without any manual input required.

🏷

Smart Categorization

Automatically assigns activities to projects based on app bundle IDs and window title keywords.

📊

Visual Timeline

Interactive timeline with zoom and pan to visualize your day and understand your work patterns.

🔒

Privacy First

All data stored locally on your Mac. No cloud sync, no accounts, no data ever leaves your device.

💰

Completely Free

No subscription fees, no in-app purchases, no ads. Open source and free forever.

⚙

Native macOS

Built with SwiftUI and SwiftData for a fast, native experience that integrates perfectly with macOS.
